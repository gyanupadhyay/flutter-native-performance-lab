# Hinglish phone walkthrough

[Watch or download the video](walkthrough-hinglish.mp4) · [Transcript](script.txt) · [Subtitles](walkthrough-hinglish.srt)

The 79-second walkthrough explains Speed and Smoothness using actual recordings from a OnePlus CPH2467 running Android 15, plus labelled screenshots of live-tour results. Video segments play at their original speed. It includes Hindi/English narration generated with HeyGen Voice (Tushar, Relaxed & Friendly), with burned-in captions.

The video's 60 fps capture rate is separate from the app's animation-ticker measurements. Its results come from a separate live-tour run; small differences from the README's repeated suite are expected. Recording also adds device load. [Live-tour animation report](../results/mobile-tour-animation-results.json).

Format: 720 x 1600, 60 fps, H.264/AAC MP4, approximately 4.7 MB. The sidecar SRT contains the same captions already burned into the video.

Decoding, duration, audio signal levels, subtitle coverage, representative frames, and phase labels were checked. Voice pronunciation and audible mix quality have not been manually auditioned.

Local production recordings, tool downloads, provider response URLs, and scratch files are excluded from the repository.
